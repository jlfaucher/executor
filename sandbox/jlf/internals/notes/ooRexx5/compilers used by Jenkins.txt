The macro HAVE_PRAGMA_GCC_STRINGOPOVERFLOW is not a standard GCC or C/C++ pre-defined macro.
It is a custom identifier typically defined by CMake or specific build systems to detect compiler support for the -Wstringop-overflow warning pragma.

CMakeLists.txt
  message(STATUS "-- determine available pragmas --")
  check_c_source_compiles("void main() {
    #pragma GCC diagnostic ignored \"-Wstringop-overflow\"
    }" HAVE_PRAGMA_GCC_STRINGOPOVERFLOW FAIL_REGEX "warning")
